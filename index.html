01Who we are

A modern undercover security company

Kathorus Security is a specialist undercover security company. We place trained agents inside businesses to find what ordinary guarding cannot see — theft, collusion and the routes stolen goods take out of your premises.

We employ PSIRA-registered security guards, run our own undercover agent training, and support classroom training with tailored on-site work. Every deployment is measured against clear internal performance indicators, so you can see whether risk is actually coming down.

Our vision — to be recognised as the global leader in protecting and reducing theft in business.

Why put an undercover agent on site?

  • To tell you things you do not already know.
  • To help you identify criminal activity as it happens.
  • To identify the culprits — including employees involved in theft.
  • To establish where stolen goods are taken from your site.
  • To trace how those goods are moved out.
  • To set traps that catch the people responsible.

04How we work

Philosophy, mission, measurement

Our philosophy

We train our own undercover agents, employ PSIRA-registered guards, and support classroom training with tailored on-site work. We deliver beyond expectation: pro-active solutions and quantifiable risk improvements.

Our mission

To meet our customers' security needs by providing integrated solutions — guarding, investigation and training working as one system rather than three suppliers.

How we measure

We use a range of internal measures to track the performance of the business and the service we deliver, so claims about reduced risk are backed by something you can review.
